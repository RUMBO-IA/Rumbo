# RUMBO IA Messaging V1

## Primary promise
ES: IA con control humano para organizar y automatizar operaciones comerciales.
EN: Human-controlled AI for organized, automated commercial operations.

## Positioning
RUMBO IA is a human-controlled AI CRM and automation system for small businesses in Argentina and Latin America. It connects conversations, leads, follow-ups, approved knowledge and bounded workflows while preserving human control over sensitive decisions.

## Product one-liners
- RUMBO CRM: one commercial history across conversations, leads and follow-ups.
- RUMBO Opportunity Engine: finds, deduplicates, enriches and scores opportunities while retaining evidence.
- RUMBO Revenue Recovery: bounded workflows for stalled leads, forgotten quotes, no-shows and dormant customers.
- RUMBO Guardian: policy and evidence controls that keep consequential actions behind explicit verification boundaries.
- RUMBO Voice: supervised voice workflows for approved information, qualification, scheduling, transfer and missed-call recovery.

## Approved CTA vocabulary
Evaluate pilot; See the system; Explore services; Review scope; Start pilot intake; Contact RUMBO IA.

## Proof vocabulary
Prefer: built, demonstrated, simulated data, pilot-ready, controlled pilot, evidence-backed, human-supervised, bounded workflow.

Avoid unless independently evidenced: best, guaranteed, autonomous, zero-risk, 100% secure, proven ROI, production-scale, enterprise-grade.

## Commercial hierarchy
Revenue Recovery Sprint — one bounded commercial workflow, 14 calendar days, USD 149 one-time before kickoff. Scope and baseline precede outcome claims. Sensitive actions remain human-supervised. No ROI guarantee.
