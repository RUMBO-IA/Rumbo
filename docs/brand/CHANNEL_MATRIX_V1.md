## Invariants
1. `RUMBO IA` is the first-mention name unless a platform field forbids spaces.
2. `RUMBO` may be used only after identity is established.
3. DEMO/PILOT/PRODUCTION/MEASURED are evidence states, not marketing adjectives.
4. No channel may promote a stronger factual state than the evidence receipt supports.
5. Publication authority and production deployment authority remain separate gates.
