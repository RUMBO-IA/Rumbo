# RUMBO Brand System V2 — Candidate

Status: `CANDIDATE` — not production authority.

## Architecture
- Parent company/ecosystem: **RUMBO**
- Primary public AI expression: **RUMBO IA**
- Technical namespace: **RUMBO-IA**
- CRM: application lane, not parent identity
- `RUMBO Labs`: excluded pending a new collision review

## Positioning
**EN:** AI systems you can operate, verify and trust.

**ES:** Sistemas de IA que podés operar, verificar y controlar.

Support line: **From agents to evidence. From automation to accountable execution.**

## Capability language
Build · Operate · Verify · Deploy

## Logo policy
A directional `R` is the company monogram candidate. Founder portraits remain personal/editorial assets and are never the company logo.

## Voice and truth rules
Concrete, technical, understandable and falsifiable. Evidence before claims. Do not invent scale, customers, revenue, security guarantees, production status or valuation.

## Commercial architecture
1. Reliability / Decision Sprint
2. Bounded implementation
3. Recurring Control Desk
4. Product subscriptions after repeatability
5. Enterprise / partner licensing after production evidence

## Support architecture
Keep open-source/community sponsorship separate from commercial purchases. Never publish test-mode payments or an unverified crypto address as live funding rails.

## Promotion gate
This document deliberately does not replace Brand System V1 by itself. Promotion requires the canonical registry/contracts to be migrated together, governed CI to pass, publication authority to be satisfied, and the existing production lock to be updated through its normal process.
