# RUMBO Community Code of Conduct

RUMBO community spaces are for technical collaboration, learning, research, and constructive product discussion.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Keep contributions inside the declared public or private collaboration boundary.
- Respect licenses, confidentiality, privacy, and intellectual-property rights.
- Report security issues through the private route in `SECURITY.md`.
- Follow maintainer decisions on scope, moderation, safety, and repository policy.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, credential sharing, publishing private data, deliberate disruption, malicious code, attempts to bypass repository controls, or using community access to obtain unauthorized access to RUMBO or third-party systems.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, reject, or remove content and may suspend collaboration access when needed to protect people, projects, systems, or the integrity of the community.

Participation in a repository or community space does not create employment, contractor, partner, customer, or production-access status.
