Service providers and subprocessors

RUMBO IA uses a small set of service providers to operate its public and customer-facing systems. The legal role of a provider may vary by engagement and data flow; a signed customer agreement controls where applicable.

  • Vercel — public web application hosting, delivery and standard service logs.
  • Supabase — authentication, workspace/tenant data, application database and tenant-scoped support records.
  • CookieYes — website consent-management tooling where loaded by the public site.
  • Google / Gmail — human business communications and partner/support correspondence where used.

AI model providers are not represented here as a universal default subprocessor. When a customer workflow uses an AI provider, that provider and the relevant data boundary should be identified in the written scope or applicable contract.

Resend is not currently represented as verified production transactional-email transport in this register.
