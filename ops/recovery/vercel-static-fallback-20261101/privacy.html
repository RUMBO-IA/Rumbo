Privacy notice

This notice describes the current public and customer-facing data flows for RUMBO IA Operational AI Systems. A signed customer agreement or DPA may add or supersede requirements for a specific engagement.

Information we may process

  • contact and business correspondence you send to RUMBO IA;
  • account, authentication, workspace and membership records used to provide customer access;
  • tenant-scoped support tickets and related case history;
  • commercial, order, billing and transaction-reference records when a paid engagement exists;
  • standard hosting, security and request logs such as IP address, browser/device information and timestamps;
  • workflow inputs, outputs or approved business knowledge only when a customer scope requires those data to operate the agreed system.

Why we use it

RUMBO IA uses information to provide the requested service, authenticate and administer workspaces, answer inquiries, handle support, maintain security and audit evidence, manage agreed commercial records, and meet applicable legal or contractual obligations. Data is not sold.

AI-provider boundary

No single AI provider is represented as a universal default processor. When a customer workflow uses an AI provider, the provider and relevant data boundary should be identified in the written scope or applicable agreement. Provider status, tool success or model output does not by itself establish customer entitlement or authority.
