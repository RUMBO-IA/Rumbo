03 / Responsible deployment

Boundaries are part of the architecture.

Human control

Operators can review, intervene, override and receive escalations.

Least privilege

Agents receive only the tools and permissions required for each task.

Traceability

Conversations, decisions and actions are recorded for review.

Provider resilience

Workloads can be evaluated and routed by quality, reliability and cost.
