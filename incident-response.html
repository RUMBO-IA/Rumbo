Incident response

RUMBO IA uses an evidence-first process for confirmed or suspected security and service incidents affecting systems within its authority.

  1. Record the observed event and affected scope without promoting assumptions to facts.
  2. Contain unsafe or ambiguous effects and preserve relevant evidence.
  3. Identify the current authority, affected tenant/service and recovery boundary.
  4. Remediate through the smallest verified change and independently read back the result where possible.
  5. Notify affected customers or counterparties when required by applicable law, contract, or the verified incident scope.
  6. Preserve a durable receipt or incident record when the event is material.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately through the GitHub Security “Report a vulnerability” channel or, if unavailable, to sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans. Do not post secrets or exploit details in public issues.

This public policy does not promise a response-time SLA unless a signed customer agreement states one.

Human control: consequential actions remain subject to explicit human authority and review within the applicable scope.
