Responsible AI

Human-controlled operational AI

RUMBO IA designs Operational AI Systems around explicit authority, least privilege, fail-closed behavior and verifiable effects.

  • Prepared actions are not treated as executed actions.
  • Execution is not treated as success until the relevant effect is read back when a reliable readback path exists.
  • Consequential customer actions require the authority and human-control boundary defined for the workflow.
  • Demos, simulations and benchmark outputs are not presented as customer outcomes.
  • Provider availability, model output or tool success does not by itself establish customer entitlement, legal authority, safety or business impact.
  • AI providers and model choices remain separable from the tenant, entitlement, support and evidence model.

RUMBO IA does not claim that a third-party AI provider has approved, endorsed or partnered with RUMBO IA unless provider-authored evidence establishes that status.
