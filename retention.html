Retention and deletion

RUMBO IA retains information only for an operational, contractual, security, dispute-resolution, accounting, or legal need. There is no single public retention period that is asserted for every data category.

  • Contact and partner correspondence: retained as needed to respond and maintain legitimate business records.
  • Account and workspace data: retained while required to provide the active service and for a reasonable closure/security period subject to applicable obligations.
  • Support tickets: retained for case handling, security evidence and service-history needs, subject to customer terms and applicable law.
  • Billing and transaction records: retained as required for contractual, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or legal obligations.
  • Security and service logs: retained according to operational need and provider configuration; this page does not claim a fixed provider-log period.
  • AI workflow inputs/outputs: retained only when the written customer scope or system design requires persistence; provider-specific retention is governed by the provider configuration and applicable agreement.

Deletion or access requests may be sent to sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans. A request may be limited where retention is required by law, security, an unresolved dispute, or a binding contract.
