RUMBO IA CRM integration terms

These terms describe the bounded public RUMBO IA CRM integration used through ChatGPT/OpenAI.

Proposal-only scope

The current public v1 provides service-readiness information and prepares reviewable CRM note, lead-stage-change, and outreach-draft request artifacts. It does not autonomously send messages, delete records, retrieve customer records, or write changes to a CRM.

Human review required

Outputs are drafts or proposals for human review. They are not final CRM actions, communications, professional advice, or a representation that any proposed action has been executed.
