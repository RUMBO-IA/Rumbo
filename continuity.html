Business continuity and recovery

RUMBO IA separates mutable source, authorized production and verified provider effects. Recovery decisions are based on current authority and readback rather than on the newest branch or deployment alone.

  • Source and control artifacts are versioned.
  • Production promotion is explicitly authorized and bound to an exact application revision and deployment.
  • Known-good deployments are preserved as rollback targets when feasible.
  • Security, privacy and brand gates run before governed promotion.
  • Recovery receipts distinguish source integration from production deployment and from verified live effect.
  • Managed hosting and data providers are used within their documented service and recovery boundaries.

RUMBO IA does not publish a universal RTO or RPO on this page. Contractual recovery targets, where offered, must be stated in the signed customer agreement for the applicable service.

Human control: consequential actions remain subject to explicit human authority and review within the applicable scope.
