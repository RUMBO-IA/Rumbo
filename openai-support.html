Contact support
01 / Support

How to request support.

Email sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans with the RUMBO product name, what you expected, what happened, the approximate time, and any non-sensitive error text that helps reproduce the issue.

Do not send passwords, API keys, session cookies, recovery codes, payment-card details, private keys, seed phrases, or other secrets.

03 / Process

Manual, bounded support.

Support requests are reviewed manually. We may ask for a minimal reproduction or additional non-sensitive diagnostic context. We do not promise a fixed response time or guaranteed resolution.
