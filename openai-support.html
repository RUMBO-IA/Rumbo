02 / Security

Do not send passwords or secrets.

Do not send passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, session cookies, recovery codes, payment-card details, private keys, seed phrases, or other secrets. Provide only the minimum non-sensitive diagnostic context needed to reproduce a problem.

Human-reviewed support.

Support requests are reviewed manually. We may ask for a minimal reproduction or additional non-sensitive diagnostic context. We do not promise a fixed response time or guaranteed resolution.
