RUMBO CRM · SUPPORT

Support for the RUMBO CRM integration.

This page covers support for the bounded RUMBO CRM app and its OpenAI/ChatGPT integration. The current public surface prepares reviewable CRM proposals and does not autonomously send messages, delete records, or modify customer data.

Email support Privacy policy
01 / Support scope

What this support channel covers.

Connection issues

Problems connecting or invoking the RUMBO CRM integration from ChatGPT.

Tool behavior

Questions about readiness checks, note proposals, lead-stage proposals, and outreach draft requests.

Privacy and data handling

Questions about the bounded public surface, data minimization, and the integration's privacy policy.

Security reports

Reports about unexpected behavior, authorization boundaries, or a potential security issue in the integration.

Publisher and OpenAI status

RUMBO CRM is published by RUMBO IA. This support page does not claim OpenAI approval, verification, endorsement, review acceptance, or publication unless independently verified.

02 / Contact

How to request support.

Email sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans and identify the issue as related to RUMBO CRM.

Include

A concise description, the action you attempted, the approximate time of the issue, and any non-sensitive error message.

Do not include

Passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, payment data, private keys, or other secrets.

Customer data

Do not send unnecessary customer records or conversation exports. Provide only the minimum context needed to reproduce the issue.

Expected boundary

The public v1 integration is proposal-only. Requests to send messages or mutate CRM records are outside the supported public surface.
