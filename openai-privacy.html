Privacy Notice for RUMBO IA OpenAI Apps

Effective: September 7, 2026

This notice applies to the RUMBO IA apps/plugins submitted for use through OpenAI products. It is intentionally limited to those integrations and does not replace privacy notices that may apply to other RUMBO IA services.

Data processed

The current v0.1 app family processes the text that a user intentionally supplies to a tool, such as a request, optional context, and optional non-secret constraints. The tools are designed not to request passwords, MFA codes, payment-card data, government identifiers, biometrics, or other credentials.

Purpose

Input is used to produce structured, read-only analysis such as facts, assumptions, next actions, and remaining gates. The current v0.1 tools do not send messages, make purchases, book travel, execute trades, publish content, or change third-party systems.

Storage and retention

The reference v0.1 MCP implementation does not include an application database or an application-level persistence mechanism for tool inputs or outputs. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs according to their own policies. RUMBO IA will avoid placing full user prompts or secrets in production logs.

Sharing

RUMBO IA does not sell personal information through these apps. Data necessarily passes through OpenAI and the infrastructure used to operate the MCP endpoint so the requested tool call can be processed. Their respective policies govern their processing.

Security and control

The app family is designed as read-only. Tool metadata declares no destructive or open-world side effects, and outputs explicitly report authority_effect=NONE. Availability of a tool does not grant authority to perform an external action.

Your choices

Do not submit secrets or highly sensitive information to these tools. You may stop using the app at any time. Privacy questions or deletion requests relating to data directly controlled by RUMBO IA may be sent to sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans.

Changes

If a future app version adds authentication, persistent storage, third-party APIs, or write actions, this notice must be updated before that version is submitted or published.
