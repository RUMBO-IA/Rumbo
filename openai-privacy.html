Privacy notice

This notice explains how RUMBO IA handles information on this public website and in the RUMBO Agent Reliability skills-only plugin. The current Agent Reliability version does not connect to an RUMBO MCP server or RUMBO backend, and RUMBO does not receive or store ChatGPT conversation content through that plugin.

Data categories and purposes. If you contact RUMBO IA, the contact details and message you provide are used to respond to your inquiry and maintain necessary business records. Standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps and basic request metadata, may be processed by hosting, security and service-operation providers to operate, secure and troubleshoot the site.

Recipients and processors. Contact information may be processed by email, hosting, security, analytics or infrastructure providers used to operate the relevant service. RUMBO IA does not sell personal information.

Retention. Contact information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or satisfy applicable legal obligations. Standard technical logs are retained according to the operational and security policies of the relevant infrastructure provider.

User controls. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by RUMBO IA, or ask questions about its processing, by contacting sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans. Where applicable, requests remain subject to legal retention requirements and verification needed to protect the requester and the service.

Plugin boundary. RUMBO Agent Reliability v0.1.6 is skills-only. It has no RUMBO MCP/backend mapping and therefore has no RUMBO-side conversation-data store. Any ChatGPT-side processing remains governed by OpenAI and the user's ChatGPT account and workspace controls.
