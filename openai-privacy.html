RUMBO CRM privacy notice

This notice covers the bounded RUMBO CRM integration published by RUMBO IA and used through ChatGPT/OpenAI as well as support contact related to that integration.

Data the integration receives

Depending on the tool you choose, the integration may receive a workspace identifier, subject or lead identifier, note text, current and proposed lead stages, a reason, outreach channel, and outreach objective. The service-readiness tool receives no customer-record input.

How the bounded public v1 uses that data

The integration uses submitted values only to validate the request and return a reviewable proposal in the same tool call. The bounded public v1 does not search or retrieve customer records, write notes or stages to a CRM, send email, SMS, or WhatsApp messages, or authorize production actions.

The current MCP adapter does not write submitted tool values to an application database or CRM. Its proposal outputs echo only the fields needed for human review and mark them REVIEW_REQUIRED with side_effect=false.

Platform and infrastructure processing

ChatGPT/OpenAI processes conversation and app-call data under its own applicable terms and privacy practices. Hosting and network providers may process standard technical records such as IP address, browser or client information, request timestamps, status codes, and security logs to operate and protect their services.

Data minimization

Do not submit passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, payment data, private keys, or unnecessary sensitive customer information. Provide only the minimum text and identifiers needed to prepare the requested proposal.

Website and support contact

If you contact RUMBO IA by email, the information you provide is used to respond to your inquiry and maintain necessary business records. It is not sold.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans.
