Support and escalation

Authenticated customers can open tenant-scoped support tickets through the RUMBO IA support portal. Current ticket categories include technical, billing, security and account issues, with normal, high and urgent severity labels.

Severity is a prioritization signal and does not itself create a contractual response-time commitment. Any response or resolution target that applies to a customer is stated in the signed scope, order or support agreement.

Customer ticket intake is separated from operator resolution authority. A submitted ticket is not treated as resolved until the corresponding support state and effect are verified.

Security vulnerabilities should use the private security-reporting route described in the Security and Incident Response pages rather than a public issue.

For business or access questions when the authenticated portal is unavailable, contact sebastian@rumbo.verso.fans.

Human control: consequential actions remain subject to explicit human authority and review within the applicable scope.
