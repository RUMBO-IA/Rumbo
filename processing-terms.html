Data processing

DPA-ready processing terms

This public summary is not a signed Data Processing Agreement. Where RUMBO IA processes personal data on behalf of a customer, the signed order, statement of work, contract, or customer-specific DPA controls.

Instructions and purpose. RUMBO IA processes customer data only for the documented service purpose and authorized workflow. Consequential actions remain subject to the agreed human-control and authority boundaries.

Confidentiality and security. Access is limited to authorized operational needs. Credentials and secrets are kept out of public source, and production changes require explicit authority, verification and readback.

Service providers. RUMBO IA may use infrastructure and business-service providers described in the service-provider register. Customer-specific AI providers or integrations are disclosed in the applicable scope where relevant.

Deletion and return. Retention and deletion follow the Retention and Deletion Policy, applicable law, and signed customer terms. A customer-specific DPA may specify additional deletion, assistance, audit, transfer, or notification obligations.

No public page substitutes for an executed DPA where one is legally or contractually required.
